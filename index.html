Keeping score

Many jobs. One stewardship score. Watch it move.

Stewardship moves as the jobs get done, across five dimensions: Timeliness, Compliance, Oversight, Reach and Impact. In the 60s, filings are late and requests sit unanswered. In the 90s, nothing is overdue and every point has a receipt. The board, each owner and the building are scored the same way, and the owners see the building's number.

Why no manager

A management company sells a checklist and a phone number. Keep the phone number if you like.

Keeping the building's long-term health on track is the real job. That means knowing what is due, running the meeting, handling the problems, keeping the books, planning for the roof and holding the records. A management company charges per unit, every month, plus fees on top, and the checklist is as far as most of them get. ONE two does all of it and your vendors get paid at their price, not a marked-up one.

The phone number is a person. Most boards find they do not need one once the checklist runs itself. If you do, add-on the On Call service to have a named advisor who knows your building and sits on your side of the table, not the manager's.
