1. Who we are and what this policy covers

ONE two LLC (“ONE two,” “we,” “us”) is a District of Columbia limited liability company. We provide governance, compliance and financial software for self-managed condominium and homeowner associations.

This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you visit getonetwo.com, sign up for a trial or subscription, or use the ONE two application (together, the “Services”).

Two roles. We handle personal information in two different capacities:

  • For our own business. Information about the people who sign up, buy subscriptions, contact us or visit our website. We decide how this information is used.
  • On behalf of associations. Information an association stores in ONE two about its units, owners, residents, vendors and board. The association (usually acting through its board) controls that information and decides who can see it. We process it to provide the Services under our agreement with the association.

If you are an owner or resident and have questions about records your association keeps in ONE two, please contact your board first. We will help your association respond.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide the Services: create accounts, run your association’s workspace, generate reports, notices, invoices and filings, and send the emails and postal mail your board asks us to send.
  • Process payments: bill subscriptions and add-ons, and let associations collect assessments and other payments from owners.
  • Calculate stewardship scores: summarize how owners and board members take part in running the building (timeliness, compliance, oversight, reach and impact) so the association can encourage good habits. Scores are not used to make legal, credit, housing or eligibility decisions about anyone.
  • Support you: answer questions, troubleshoot problems and, if your association subscribes to On Call, provide advisory support.
  • Keep the Services secure and working: authenticate users, enforce access permissions, detect fraud and abuse, monitor errors and fix bugs.
  • Improve the Services: understand which features are used and where people get stuck. Where practical we use aggregated or de-identified data for this.
  • Measure our advertising: learn whether our ads in ChatGPT lead people to start a trial, as described in Information we collect. We never use association records for this.
  • Communicate with you: send service messages (trial reminders, billing notices, security alerts) and, if you have not opted out, news about ONE two.
  • Meet legal obligations: keep financial and tax records, respond to lawful requests and enforce our Terms.

Association data stays the association’s. We use the records an association stores in ONE two only to provide the Services to that association, as described above. We do not sell them, use them to market to owners on our own behalf, or combine one association’s records with another’s except in aggregated, de-identified form.

AI features. Some features may use an AI model to answer questions or draft content. When you use them, your prompt and the building context needed to answer it are sent to our AI provider to generate a response. We do not permit our AI provider to train its models on this data.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information for money. We share limited information with OpenAI to measure our ads, as described below, and you can opt out.

Within your association. What other users can see depends on their role and the association’s settings. Board members can generally see unit, owner, financial and governance records. Owners and residents see their own records plus whatever the board chooses to publish (for example, the directory, announcements, meeting materials or a building-wide stewardship average). Ballots and votes recorded in ONE two are visible to the board members who administer the election.

5. How long we keep information

We keep account and association information for as long as the association’s subscription is active. Associations often must keep financial, election and meeting records for years under their governing documents and local law, so boards can export records at any time.

After a subscription ends, we keep association data for 90 days so the board can export it, then delete or de-identify it, except where we must keep certain records (such as our own billing and tax records) to meet legal obligations. Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule.

6. How we protect information

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit, role-based access controls that separate each association’s data, hashed passwords and access logging. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the affected association as required by law.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction. You can view and update most of your account information in the app. Ask your board to correct association records about you.
  • Deletion. You can ask us to delete your account. Records the association must keep (for example, ledger entries or meeting minutes that name you) may remain with the association.
  • Email. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in each message. You will still receive service and billing messages.
  • Opting out of advertising measurement. We honor the Global Privacy Control signal. If your browser sends it, we tell the OpenAI pixel not to measure your visit, and we do not send OpenAI your hashed email address when you start a trial. You can turn the signal on in your browser’s privacy settings or with a browser extension. Blocking third-party cookies or using an ad blocker also limits the pixel.
  • State privacy rights. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or its sharing for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money; to opt out of advertising measurement, use the Global Privacy Control signal described above. To make a request, email us at privacy@getonetwo.com. We will verify your identity before responding and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If your request concerns records we hold for an association, we will forward it to that association and help it respond. You may use an authorized agent, and you may appeal a decision by replying to our response.

8. Children

The Services are for adults who own, live in or manage property. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Where we process information

ONE two is based in the United States, and our service providers store and process information in the United States.
